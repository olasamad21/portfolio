Process

How I design

Structure before style. Every decision traces back to what the user needs at that step.

01

Research & flows

Map the journey before touching a screen: who the user is and what they need at each step.

02

Low-fidelity wireframes

Structure and hierarchy first, with no colour or type decisions yet.

03

High-fidelity screens

Real type, colour and spacing. This is where the screens start to look shippable.

04

Interactive prototype

Linked, clickable flows in Figma for testing and stakeholder review.
